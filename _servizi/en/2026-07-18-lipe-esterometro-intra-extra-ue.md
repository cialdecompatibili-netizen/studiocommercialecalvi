<p>LIPE and the esterometro are two distinct VAT obligations, often confused: LIPE concerns periodic settlements, the former esterometro concerns cross-border transactions. Handling them well, with the right deadlines, avoids penalties that can reach 2,000 euros for each omitted or incorrect communication.</p><h2 id="lipe-the-mandatory-quarterly-communication">LIPE: the mandatory quarterly communication</h2><p>The Communication of Periodic VAT Settlements (introduced by Decree-Law 78/2010, art. 21-bis) requires VAT taxable persons to send each quarter the summary data of their settlements, by the last day of the second month following the reference quarter. The obligation remains even when the settlement is in credit or zero, if there is data to report.</p><p><strong>2026 deadlines:</strong></p><ul><li>1st quarter (Jan-Mar): 1 June 2026</li><li>2nd quarter (Apr-Jun): 30 September 2026</li><li>3rd quarter (Jul-Sep): 30 November 2026</li><li>4th quarter (Oct-Dec): 1 March 2027</li></ul><p>For the fourth quarter, sending the LIPE can be avoided if the data flows directly into the annual VAT return filed by the same deadline, correctly completing section VP (paying attention to line VP9, where any VAT credit from the previous year must be shown).</p><h2 id="esterometro-now-inside-e-invoicing">Esterometro: now inside e-invoicing</h2><p>A frequent mistake is confusing the Intrastat obligation with the transmission of cross-border transaction data (the old esterometro), now integrated into the e-invoicing system through document codes TD17, TD18 and TD19. They are obligations with different purposes: Intrastat is statistical, transmission to the Sistema di Interscambio is fiscal. Any exemption from the Intra 2-bis form (threshold raised to 2 million euros in 2026) does not remove the obligation to report intra-Community purchase invoices via SdI.</p><h2 id="penalties">Penalties</h2><p>Omitted, late or incorrect transmission of the LIPE carries a penalty of 500 to 2,000 euros per communication. If you notice late, ravvedimento operoso can drastically reduce the penalty (even down to about 27.78 euros if you act quickly) — it is best to act immediately, not wait for an audit.</p><h2 id="how-we-help">How we help</h2><p>We keep all the quarterly LIPE deadlines under control and the correct handling of cross-border transactions via SdI, checking that the operating flow (invoicing, SdI, esterometro) is always consistent with what is declared — a return that is formally correct but disconnected from the real flow remains at risk in case of a Revenue Agency audit.</p>
