<p>Managing staff does not just mean paying salaries on time: every stage of the employment relationship, from hiring to severance pay to any dismissal, has precise rules that, if not followed, expose the company to disputes that can be costly. Knowing them beforehand avoids having to learn them in court.</p><h2 id="assunzione-la-scelta-del-contratto-giusto">Hiring: choosing the right contract</h2><p>Permanent, fixed-term, apprenticeship or agency work: each contract type has specific constraints on duration, grounds and labour cost. The choice must also take into account the obligations that arise over time, such as the renewal limits for fixed-term contracts.</p><h2 id="tfr-come-si-calcola-e-si-gestisce">Severance pay (TFR): how it is calculated and managed</h2><p>Severance pay (TFR) accrues each year as a share of pay and must be revalued annually according to ISTAT indices. The employer must decide, together with the employee, whether to keep it in the company or direct it to a supplementary pension fund.</p><h2 id="licenziamento-le-regole-da-rispettare">Dismissal: the rules to follow</h2><p>A dismissal must always be justified by a specific ground and communicated in the ways required by law. The protections that apply vary according to the size of the company and the employee's hiring date.</p><div class="art-nota"><p>Resignations also follow a formal online procedure: the documentation gathered during the relationship is often what makes the difference in the event of a dispute.</p></div>
