<p>PEC, SPID and Digital Signature are not optional for anyone with a VAT number: they are the tools with which your business officially communicates with the Public Administration. Activating them properly, and not just because they are mandatory, saves time on every future filing.</p><h2 id="pec-mail-with-legal-value">PEC: mail with legal value</h2><p>Certified Electronic Mail (PEC) is mandatory for businesses and professionals registered in an order, and is the official channel for communications with the Public Administration: it has the same legal value as registered mail with return receipt, with certified proof of sending and delivery.</p><h2 id="spid-and-digital-signature">SPID and Digital Signature</h2><p>SPID is the access key to dozens of online services: tax drawer, INPS portal, e-invoicing, regional portals. The Digital Signature, on the other hand, allows documents to be signed with the same legal value as a handwritten signature, indispensable for contracts, financial statements and official communications requiring a formal signature.</p><h2 id="online-proxies">Online proxies</h2><p>Delegating your accountant on the Entratel, Fisconline and INPS portals speeds up the submission of returns, consultation of the tax drawer and handling of communications, without having to hand over your personal credentials every time.</p><div class="art-nota"><p>Activating these tools before you urgently need them (an approaching deadline, a communication to send right away) avoids being stuck just when you need them most.</p></div>
