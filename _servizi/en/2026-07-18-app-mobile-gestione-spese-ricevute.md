<p>Lost receipts, expense reports filled in by hand, receipts that reach the firm weeks later: traditional business expense management is still one of the most inefficient activities in most small and medium-sized businesses. An expense management app with an OCR scanner digitalises the process at the source, not downstream.</p><h2 id="come-funziona-lo-scanner-ocr">How the OCR scanner works</h2><p>Simply photograph a receipt with your smartphone: optical character recognition (OCR) technology automatically extracts the date, amount, supplier and expense category, eliminating manual data entry. The more advanced apps also automatically categorise the type of expense (transport, meals, accommodation) and generate the expense report directly, with no input from the user beyond taking the photo.</p><h2 id="cosa-cambia-per-l-azienda">What changes for the business</h2><p>Having an up-to-date, real-time picture of all expenses incurred by owners, employees and collaborators — instead of finding out at the end of the month when the pile of receipts arrives — allows much more precise management control. The typical flow is: photo of the receipt → automatic data extraction → categorisation → expense report creation → manager approval → submission to accounting. Each step reduces the risk of transcription errors, duplicates or lost documents.</p><h2 id="il-beneficio-per-lo-studio-contabile">The benefit for the accounting firm</h2><p>When expenses arrive already digitised and categorised, the bookkeeping work shrinks drastically: there is no need to physically collect receipts, decipher handwriting or reconstruct after the fact the nature of an expense. The data arrives ready for integration into the accounting software, often with a direct link to the main accounting packages.</p><h2 id="sicurezza-dei-dati">Data security</h2><p>Professional expense management apps invest in end-to-end encryption from the moment of scanning to the servers, two-factor authentication and regular security updates — an aspect to always check before adopting a tool that handles the company's sensitive financial data.</p><h2 id="come-integriamo-questi-strumenti-nel-lavoro-con-i-clienti">How we integrate these tools into our work with clients</h2><p>We help our clients choose and configure the expense management app best suited to their business, connect the digitised flow directly to our accounting software, and monitor that every recorded expense is correctly positioned for tax purposes — turning a previously scattered activity into a process that can be traced and checked at any time.</p>
