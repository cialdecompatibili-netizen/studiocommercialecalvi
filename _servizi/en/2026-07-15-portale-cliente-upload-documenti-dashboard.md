<p>Managing invoices, receipts and tax documents by email or on paper causes delays and lost documents: a digital client portal centralises everything in a single space accessible at any time.</p><h2 id="caricamento-documenti">Document upload</h2><p>Invoices, bank statements, receipts and contracts are uploaded directly to the portal, organised by period and type, avoiding scattered email attachments or messy shared folders.</p><h2 id="dashboard-di-controllo">Control dashboard</h2><p>An overview shows upcoming deadlines, payments to be made and the status of ongoing matters, so you do not discover a tax deadline only when it is already close.</p><h2 id="comunicazione-diretta">Direct communication</h2><p>Requests for clarification, missing documents and updates on the status of matters all go through the same channel, with a searchable history instead of emails scattered over time.</p><h2 id="firma-digitale-integrata">Integrated digital signature</h2><p>Documents that require a signature (powers of attorney, engagement letters, minutes) are signed directly from the portal with a remote electronic signature, without printing, signing by hand and rescanning: the client signs from a smartphone or computer and immediately receives the signed copy, with full legal validity.</p><h2 id="conservazione-a-norma">Legally compliant storage</h2><p>Electronic invoices and other tax-relevant documents uploaded to the portal are stored in line with the AgID Guidelines, with a minimum retention of 10 years as required by the civil code: a mandatory requirement that a simple email archive or shared folder does not satisfy.</p><div class="art-nota"><p>A well-organised portal significantly reduces the time spent looking for a past document or finding out where a matter stands.</p></div>
