<p>Enrolment in the special section of the Business Register is the step that makes innovative startup status operational: having an innovative idea is not enough, a formal filing is needed declaring that the requirements set by law are met, verifiable in case of inspection.</p><h2 id="who-can-enrol">Who can enrol</h2><p>Limited companies (typically S.r.l. or S.r.l.s., including cooperatives), unlisted, whose corporate purpose is exclusively or mainly the development, production and marketing of innovative products or services of high technological value. All the "cumulative" requirements of art. 25 of Decree-Law 179/2012 are needed (including: no profit distribution, incorporated less than 60 months ago) plus at least one of the three "alternative" innovation requirements — the most common among Italian startups is R&amp;D spending above 15% of the greater of costs and revenue.</p><p>For newly formed startups without financial statements yet, this requirement is shown by a forecast declaration signed by the legal representative, then verified in the first filed financial statements.</p><h2 id="how-to-file-the-application">How to file the application</h2><p>Fully digital, through the Chambers of Commerce's Telemaco portal with the Comunicazione Unica (form S5, or form A for first enrolment). You need the legal representative's digital signature, an active company PEC and documentation in PDF/A. For simultaneous incorporations, the code "O" (ordinary enrolment) and "U" (special section) can travel in the same filing. Average time: 2-4 weeks from signing the deed of incorporation to actual enrolment.</p><p><strong>Beware of self-certification:</strong> it is not just any formal obligation. Declaring requirements you do not meet exposes you to problems in case of inspection, with loss of status and possible challenge to incentives already used.</p><h2 id="after-enrolment-annual-maintenance">After enrolment: annual maintenance</h2><p>Status is not permanent: every year, after approval of the financial statements and by 30 June, a Comunicazione Unica confirming the requirements are still met must be sent (form B, or C/C1/D depending on how long you have been enrolled). If the confirmation is not filed, the Chamber of Commerce removes the startup from the special section ex officio, with loss of all related incentives.</p><h2 id="time-window">Time window</h2><p>Enrolment must be made within 5 years of the company's incorporation. Note: if enrolment happens more than 2 years after incorporation, the remaining duration of status in the special section is automatically reduced — the sooner you enrol, the longer you benefit from the incentives.</p><h2 id="how-we-help">How we help</h2><p>We check that the corporate purpose and requirements are actually consistent before filing the self-certification, prepare the ComUnica filing and handle the annual confirmation of the requirements, so you do not risk ex officio removal over a missed deadline.</p>
