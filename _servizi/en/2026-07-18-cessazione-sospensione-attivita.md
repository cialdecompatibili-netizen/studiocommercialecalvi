<p>Those who stop their business do not always have to close it for good: in some cases a temporary suspension is a better solution than closing the VAT number.</p><h2 id="cessazione-definitiva">Permanent closure</h2><p>It involves closing the VAT number with a notice to the Revenue Agency and, if applicable, deregistration from the Business Register. It is the right choice when the business will not be resumed.</p><h2 id="sospensione-temporanea">Temporary suspension</h2><p>Some businesses, in particular those registered with the Business Register, can be suspended for a limited period without formally closing the VAT number, useful for interruptions due to maternity, illness or other temporary causes.</p><h2 id="i-termini-da-rispettare-per-la-cessazione">Deadlines for closure</h2><p>The closure notice must be submitted within 30 days of the actual end of operations (art. 35, Presidential Decree 633/1972): form AA9/12 for professionals, through the Revenue Agency's online services; the ComUnica procedure for sole proprietorships, which also automatically closes the position with INPS, INAIL and the Business Register. Since 2016 the Revenue Agency no longer applies penalties for late VAT notification, but beware: INAIL and the Chamber of Commerce can still apply their own penalties (roughly between 125 and 770 euros for INAIL, about 200 euros for the Chamber of Commerce) for delay in the respective notice.</p><h2 id="gli-obblighi-da-rispettare-comunque">Obligations that apply in any case</h2><p>Whether you close or suspend, the tax formalities for the period of activity carried out still have to be handled: the VAT return and income tax return for the last tax year (anyone closing in 2026 will file the 2027 Redditi return), plus payment of the social security contributions accrued up to the closing date.</p><div class="art-nota"><p>The choice between suspending and closing depends on the prospect of resuming the business: a correct assessment avoids having to reopen from scratch a VAT number closed by mistake.</p></div>
