<p>Anyone who carries on a trading or craft business on their own account, habitually and mainly, must register with the relevant INPS social security scheme, regardless of the tax regime chosen for their VAT number.</p><h2 id="chi-deve-iscriversi">Who must register</h2><p>The Traders' Scheme covers those engaged in retail, catering or intermediation; the Craftspeople's Scheme covers those producing goods or services with mainly their own labour, even with the help of collaborators.</p><h2 id="quanto-si-paga-nel-2026">How much you pay in 2026</h2><p>According to INPS Circular no. 14/2026, the minimum income for 2026 is 18,808 euros. The compulsory annual fixed contribution (due even with zero income or a loss) is about 4,521 euros for craftspeople and 4,612 euros for traders. On the portion of income above the minimum, up to 56,224 euros, a rate of 24% applies (craftspeople) or 24.48% (traders, which includes the additional 0.48% for the business cessation indemnity); above 56,224 euros the rate rises by one percentage point.</p><h2 id="riduzione-del-35-per-i-forfettari">35% reduction for flat-rate taxpayers</h2><p>Those who apply the flat-rate regime can ask INPS, online, for a 35% reduction on the total contributions due, both on the minimum and on the excess. It is not automatic: for those already in business the application must normally be submitted by 28 February of the reference year; new registrants submit it together with their registration.</p><h2 id="tempistiche-e-obblighi">Timing and obligations</h2><p>Registration must be done together with opening the VAT number or registering with the Business Register, with quarterly payments for the fixed portion (indicative deadlines: May, August, November, and February of the following year) and a balancing payment with the income tax return for the variable portion.</p><div class="art-nota"><p>Contributions paid to these schemes are in any case deductible from total income, reducing the tax due in the same year in which they are paid.</p></div>
