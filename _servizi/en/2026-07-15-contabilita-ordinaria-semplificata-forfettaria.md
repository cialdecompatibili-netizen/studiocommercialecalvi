<p>Your bookkeeping regime determines which books you must keep, how taxable income is calculated and how many periodic filings are required: it is not a personal preference but a consequence of your legal form and revenue.</p><h2 id="contabilita-forfettaria">Flat-rate bookkeeping</h2><p>Reserved for individuals with revenue within the legal threshold, it calculates income by applying a profitability coefficient to turnover, without deducting costs line by line. Filings are minimal, but there is no VAT deduction on purchases.</p><h2 id="contabilita-semplificata">Simplified bookkeeping</h2><p>The natural regime for sole proprietorships and partnerships below the legal revenue threshold. Income is calculated on a cash basis (receipts minus payments), with VAT registers and a summary statement, and no obligation to prepare financial statements.</p><h2 id="contabilita-ordinaria">Ordinary bookkeeping</h2><p>Compulsory for limited companies and for anyone exceeding the revenue thresholds, or chosen voluntarily. It requires a general journal, an inventory book, annual financial statements and line-by-line tracking of every component of income and assets.</p><div class="art-nota"><p>Exceeding a revenue threshold during the year can trigger a mandatory move to a higher regime the following year: it is worth monitoring your figures before the deadline suddenly arrives.</p></div>
