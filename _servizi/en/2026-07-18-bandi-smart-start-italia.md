<p>Smart&amp;Start Italia is the main subsidised finance tool managed by Invitalia to support the launch and growth of innovative, high-tech startups across the country. It has already financed over 1,500 startups, with more than 618 million euros paid out.</p><h2 id="quanto-finanzia">How much it finances</h2><p>It covers eligible expenses between 100,000 and 1,500,000 euros, with a zero-interest loan and no collateral:</p><ul><li><strong>80%</strong> of expenses, for all admitted startups</li><li><strong>90%</strong> if the startup is made up entirely of women, under-36s, or partners with a PhD who have returned to Italy</li></ul><p>In Southern Italy the terms are even more favourable, with a share of non-repayable grant on top of the subsidised loan.</p><h2 id="chi-pu-accedere">Who can apply</h2><p>Innovative startups registered in the special section of the Business Register, incorporated less than 60 months ago, based in Italy (foreign teams with a Startup Visa are also admitted), with a value of production below 5 million euros. The project must have at least one of these characteristics:</p><ul><li>economic exploitation of research results (public or private)</li><li>strong technological and innovative content</li><li>focus on the digital economy, artificial intelligence, blockchain or IoT</li></ul><h2 id="come-si-presenta-domanda">How to apply</h2><p>Online through the Invitalia reserved area, attaching a business plan, financial plan and the partners' CVs. Applications are assessed in chronological order of arrival ("first come, first served", no fixed deadline), with the outcome communicated within 60 days after a formal check of the requirements and a merit assessment of the business plan. Once admitted, you have 24 months from signing the contract to carry out the project, with payments in instalments based on progress (maximum 5 progress stages).</p><h2 id="l-errore-pi-comune-che-porta-al-rigetto">The most common mistake that leads to rejection</h2><p>A generic business plan, with vague numbers or financial forecasts not backed by realistic assumptions, is the first reason for rejection. Invitalia assesses the plan's sustainability on its merits, not just the form of the application.</p><h2 id="come-ti-aiutiamo">How we help you</h2><p>We check whether your startup meets the requirements, build a solid and credible business plan for Invitalia's assessment, and follow you through managing the progress stages until the loan is fully paid out.</p>
