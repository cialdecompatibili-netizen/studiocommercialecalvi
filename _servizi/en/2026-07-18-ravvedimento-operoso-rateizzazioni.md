<p>A forgotten or insufficient payment does not have to wait for a tax assessment: ravvedimento operoso allows you to regularise spontaneously, paying the tax due with a reduced penalty plus interest — before formal checks or audits begin. The benefit is greatest if you act straight away: the longer it takes, the higher the penalty percentage.</p><h2 id="the-updated-percentages-violations-from-1-september-2024">The updated percentages (violations from 1 September 2024)</h2><p>The base penalty for omitted or late payment fell from 30% to 25% of the unpaid tax, with progressive reductions based on days of delay:</p><ul><li><strong>Within 14 days</strong> ("sprint ravvedimento") — about 0.0833% for each day of delay</li><li><strong>From the 15th to the 30th day</strong> — penalty reduced to 1.25% (fixed 1/10)</li><li><strong>From the 31st to the 90th day</strong> — penalty reduced to 1/9 of the base</li><li><strong>Over 90 days, within one year</strong> — reduction to 1/8</li><li>Further reductions (1/7, 1/6) for longer ravvedimenti, beyond one year</li></ul><p>For violations committed before 1 September 2024 the old percentages remain valid, calculated on a 30% base.</p><h2 id="statutory-interest-2026-rate">Statutory interest: 2026 rate</h2><p>From 1 January 2026 the statutory interest rate fell to 1.60% per year (it was 2% in 2025), under the Ministry of Economy decree of 10 December 2025. Interest is calculated day by day on the amount of tax due, and if the delay spans more than one calendar year it must be split applying the correct rate for each period.</p><h2 id="how-to-pay">How to pay</h2><p>Ravvedimento is completed with a single payment via form F24: tax, reduced penalty and interest must be shown on separate lines, with distinct tax codes. Payment by instalments is not allowed — if only part of the amount due is paid, the ravvedimento is not completed and the violation remains open.</p><h2 id="when-ravvedimento-is-no-longer-possible">When ravvedimento is no longer possible</h2><p>You can no longer use ravvedimento operoso if the violation has already been formally notified or if inspections, audits or other administrative activities of which the taxpayer has had formal notice have already begun. In that case other routes remain — accertamento con adesione, agreed settlement of the notice — but with less favourable penalty reductions.</p><h2 id="instalment-plans-a-different-tool">Instalment plans: a different tool</h2><p>If the debt is already entered on the collection roll (tax collection notice), an instalment plan is a separate institution from ravvedimento: it allows payment to be spread over several monthly instalments, but does not reduce the penalty already applied. It must be assessed case by case, together with any agreed settlement schemes active in the period.</p><h2 id="how-we-handle-ravvedimento-for-our-clients">How we handle ravvedimento for our clients</h2><p>We monitor payment deadlines to catch any omissions promptly, calculate the reduced penalty and correct interest based on the actual days of delay, and prepare the F24 with separate tax codes — so as to close the position in the most favourable way possible before a formal audit steps in.</p>
