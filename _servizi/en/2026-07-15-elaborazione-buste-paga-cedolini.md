<p>Every month the employer must correctly calculate pay, tax and social security deductions, and pay what is due by precise deadlines: a payroll error can lead to disputes both with the employee and with the social security bodies.</p><h2 id="cosa-contiene-il-cedolino">What a payslip contains</h2><p>Gross pay, personal income tax (IRPEF) deductions with the related surcharges, contributions payable by the employee and the employer, and any additional items such as overtime, untaken holidays or bonuses: each item follows calculation rules specific to the applicable national collective labour agreement (CCNL).</p><h2 id="adempimenti-mensili">Monthly filings</h2><p>The employer pays INPS contributions and, where applicable, INAIL contributions by the 16th of the following month, submits the UNIEMENS flows with each employee's pay and contribution data, and handles tax withholdings according to the progressive IRPEF rates.</p><h2 id="tfr-e-voci-differite">Severance pay (TFR) and deferred items</h2><p>Each month a portion of severance pay (TFR) accrues, is set aside and revalued every year, and is paid out when the employment ends or, at the employee's request, directed to supplementary pension schemes.</p><div class="art-nota"><p>Applying the correct national collective agreement from the moment of hiring avoids complex recalculations later: the collective agreement determines pay levels, seniority increments and many of the additional items on the payslip.</p></div>
