<p>Setting up an innovative startup does not mean opening just any SRL: it is a special qualification, recorded in a dedicated section of the Business Register, that unlocks tax, social security and credit-access benefits reserved for those who meet precise requirements from the deed of incorporation onwards.</p><h2 id="srl-o-srls-quale-forma-scegliere">SRL or SRLS: which form to choose</h2><p>The SRLS (simplified SRL) reduces notary costs and allows token share capital, but imposes standard, non-modifiable articles: suited to those starting with limited resources and a few like-minded partners. The ordinary SRL costs more to set up but allows tailor-made articles, essential as soon as you expect shareholders' agreements, different classes of shares or outside investors.</p><h2 id="i-requisiti-per-la-qualifica-di-startup-innovativa">Requirements for innovative startup status</h2><p>Besides the company form, the law requires: a corporate purpose that is mainly the development, production and marketing of innovative products or services with high technological value; a company incorporated less than 60 months ago; a head office in Italy or the EU with a production unit in Italy; no distribution of profits; and at least one of the following: R&amp;D expenses equal to 15% of the greater of costs and value of production, a qualified workforce (one third PhD students/researchers or two thirds holders of master's degrees), or ownership of a registered patent or software.</p><h2 id="l-iscrizione-e-i-suoi-effetti-pratici">Registration and its practical effects</h2><p>The status is obtained by registering in the special section of the Business Register, through a self-declaration by the legal representative and an electronic filing. From that moment the company benefits from exemptions on the chamber of commerce fee and stamp duty, departures from ordinary company law (for example stock option plans and fundraising through equity crowdfunding platforms) and from simplified insolvency procedures in case of crisis. The status must be renewed every year by filing the annual statement and lapses automatically once the 60 months or the turnover limit are exceeded.</p><div class="art-nota"><p>Choosing the right company form and checking the requirements before the deed of incorporation avoids having to restructure the company later, when partners or investors are already involved.</p></div>
