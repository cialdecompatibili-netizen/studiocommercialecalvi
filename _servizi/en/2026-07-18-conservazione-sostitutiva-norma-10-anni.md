<h2 id="archiviare-non-e-conservare-a-norma">Archiving is not compliant storage</h2><p>A widespread mistake is to believe an invoice is already "stored" just because it was sent to the SdI, saved on a hard disk or archived in the cloud. These steps are simple archiving, not compliant storage: without a regulated technical process that applies a digital signature and time stamp to make the document unalterable, the file can lose its evidential value in the event of a tax audit. Compliant storage is a distinct and mandatory process, not a synonym for "saving somewhere".</p><h2 id="perché-serve-un-processo-specifico">Why a specific process is needed</h2><p>Electronic invoices cannot be printed and filed in a binder, nor simply saved as files on a PC: the rules (Ministerial Decree of 17 June 2014, art. 2220 of the Civil Code, art. 39 of Presidential Decree 633/1972) require that tax-relevant electronic documents have characteristics of unalterability, integrity, authenticity and legibility guaranteed over time. This is achieved only through a technical process regulated by the Digital Administration Code and the AgID technical rules, with the periodic production of digitally signed and time-stamped storage packages.</p><h2 id="10-anni-di-conservazione-obbligatoria">10 years of mandatory storage</h2><p>Electronic invoices, both issued and received, must be stored digitally for at least 10 years from the date of issue. The deadline for completing the storage process does not coincide with the end of the tax year: the law gives three months after the deadline for filing the income tax return for that year. For example, for 2024 documents, with the income tax return filed by 31 October 2025, the final deadline for compliant storage falls on 31 January 2026.</p><h2 id="obbligo-esteso-anche-ai-forfettari">Obligation extended to flat-rate taxpayers too</h2><p>Since 2024 the obligation of compliant storage applies to all VAT number holders, including flat-rate taxpayers, with no more exceptions linked to the tax regime adopted. Those operating under the favourable regime must therefore equip themselves with the same compliant storage tools required of businesses under the ordinary regime, as there is no longer any specific exemption for this category.</p><h2 id="come-conservare-servizio-gratuito-o-provider-privati">How to store: free service or private providers</h2><p>There are two main routes. The first is the free service provided by the Revenue Agency through the Invoices and Receipts portal, which automatically archives documents that passed through the SdI, applying the required signatures and time stamps. The second is signing up with private AgID-accredited providers, often integrated into management software. In both cases a storage manager must be identified, who can be the business owner or a delegate. Beware: failure to store exposes you to penalties of 1,000 to 8,000 euros and, in an audit, documents not stored in compliance are treated as if they did not exist, with the risk of an induced assessment.</p><p><em>The deadlines and technical requirements for compliant storage are subject to periodic updates: always check your situation with an accountant or with the supplier of your management software.</em></p>
