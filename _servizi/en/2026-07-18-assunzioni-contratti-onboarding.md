<p>Hiring an employee does not end with signing the contract: it requires a sequence of precise formalities, with strict deadlines, that must be met from the day before the employment starts. A mistake at this stage can result, even much later, in penalties or disputes.</p><h2 id="la-comunicazione-obbligatoria-unilav-la-prima-scadenza-da-non-sbagliare">The mandatory UNILAV notice: the first deadline not to miss</h2><p>Even before the employee starts work, the company must submit the UNILAV form to the Employment Centre through the regional Mandatory Notices online system. The deadline is tight: the notice must be sent by midnight of the day before the employment begins. A late or missing notice exposes the company to administrative penalties and, in the most serious cases, brings to light a situation of irregular work. Submission can be delegated to an employment consultant or another authorised intermediary, but responsibility for the formality always stays with the employer.</p><p>Since 1 July 2026 the SIISL (Information System for Social and Labour Inclusion) has become operational, integrating the Mandatory Notices with other functions: the hiring notice can now start directly from a job offer published on the portal, from the candidate's CV or from an expression of interest, with automatic redirection to the CO Portal to fill in the UNILAV. For subsidised hires linked to bonuses, the announcement and the notice follow the same path — an integration that simplifies traceability but requires updating the internal procedures of the firm or company.</p><h2 id="cosa-consegnare-al-dipendente-al-momento-dell-assunzione">What to give the employee at the time of hiring</h2><p>Besides the employment contract, the employer must provide the employee, from the start of the relationship, with the essential information on the employment relationship required by Legislative Decree 104/2022 (the so-called transparency decree): duties, pay, working hours, length of the probation period and other essential conditions. A copy of the UNILAV notice must also be handed over, and the new employee must fill in the form for tax deductions and family dependants, as well as choose within six months where their severance pay (TFR) goes (keep it in the company or direct it to supplementary pension schemes).</p><h2 id="gli-adempimenti-previdenziali-e-assicurativi">Social security and insurance formalities</h2><p>Alongside the notice to the Employment Centre, the company must complete the INPS formalities to ensure correct social security coverage from the first day of work, in addition to the safety obligations (training, health surveillance where required) which also run from the start of the relationship, not from a later point.</p><h2 id="una-nota-per-chi-in-regime-forfettario">A note for those under the flat-rate regime</h2><p>Even a freelancer under the flat-rate regime can hire employees, but must pay attention to the limits on staff spending: exceeding certain thresholds can mean leaving the flat-rate regime. Before hiring, a simulation that checks the impact on the tax regime is therefore useful, not just the direct cost of the new hire.</p><h2 id="come-possiamo-aiutarti">How we can help</h2><p>We help you manage the entire hiring process: drafting the contract, sending the UNILAV on time, handing over the mandatory documents to the employee and checking the social security formalities, so you start the employment relationship without the risk of avoidable penalties.</p>
