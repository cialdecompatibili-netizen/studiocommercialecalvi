<p>Choosing the right company form affects the partners' personal liability, the minimum capital required, the annual filings and your ability to bring in new partners or investors in the future.</p><h2 id="srl-e-srls">SRL and SRLS</h2><p>In both, the partners' liability is limited to the capital they contributed. The SRLS has a standard, non-modifiable set of articles and token capital; the ordinary SRL costs more but allows tailor-made articles, useful with several partners or investors.</p><h2 id="snc-e-sas">SNC and SAS</h2><p>In an SNC all partners are jointly and severally liable without limit for the company's debts, including with their personal assets. In a SAS only the general partners have unlimited liability, while limited partners risk only the capital they contributed but cannot manage the company.</p><h2 id="spa">SPA</h2><p>Designed for larger projects: higher minimum capital, structured governance (shareholders' meeting, board, possibly a board of statutory auditors) and the ability to raise capital through shares, including listed ones.</p><div class="art-nota"><p>A company form can be changed along the way through a conversion, but it takes time and money: it is better to choose from the start with your intended destination in mind, not just your current situation.</p></div>
