<p>In December 2026 two dates really matter for a VAT-registered business: <strong>Wednesday 16 December</strong> (November VAT, withholdings, IMU balance) and <strong>Monday 28 December</strong> (VAT advance payment, because the 27th falls on a Sunday). Flat-rate taxpayers have far fewer, since they do not settle VAT. Here is the calendar with who owes what, and a checklist to close the year without surprises.</p><h2 id="16-december">Wednesday 16 December</h2><ul><li><strong>Monthly VAT for November</strong> (tax code 6011), for taxpayers settling VAT monthly.</li><li><strong>Withholdings</strong> made in November on employee and freelance payments, and employees' INPS contributions, for those acting as withholding agents.</li><li><strong>2026 IMU balance</strong>, for owners of property other than a non-luxury main home, for example offices or warehouses.</li><li><strong>Last instalment</strong>, in general, for those who spread the balance and first advance of their tax return over instalments.</li></ul><p>Quarterly VAT has no December deadline: the third-quarter settlement closed on 16 November.</p><h2 id="28-december">Monday 28 December: VAT advance payment</h2><p>The ordinary deadline is 27 December, but in 2026 it falls on a Sunday and moves to the next working day, <strong>28 December</strong>. The VAT advance applies to VAT taxpayers, monthly and quarterly:</p><ul><li><strong>Code 6013</strong> for monthly taxpayers, <strong>6035</strong> for quarterly ones.</li><li>If the amount due is below 103.29 euro, nothing is paid.</li><li>It is calculated with one of three methods: <strong>historical</strong>, <strong>forecast</strong> or <strong>analytical</strong> (on transactions up to 20 December). In every case 88% of the reference amount is paid.</li></ul><p>Choose the method before 20 December: the analytical method needs invoices and transactions recorded up to that date. Monthly Intrastat lists for November, for those who must file them, are due the same 28 December.</p><h2 id="flat-rate">What about flat-rate taxpayers?</h2><p>The flat-rate regime does not apply VAT, so no periodic settlements, no LIPE and <strong>no VAT advance</strong>. With no employees and no withheld fees, there are no withholdings either. In December a flat-rate taxpayer usually has little or nothing to pay: the second tax advance was already due on 30 November (see the <a href="{{ '/en/blog/tax-returns/second-acconto-30-november-2026/' | relative_url }}">guide to the second acconto</a>) and the fourth-quarter stamp duty is due in early March 2027 (see the <a href="{{ '/en/blog/e-invoicing/stamp-duty-electronic-invoices-how-to-pay/' | relative_url }}">guide to stamp duty on e-invoices</a>).</p><h2 id="checklist">Checklist to complete by 31 December</h2><ul><li><strong>Check revenue collected this year</strong> against the 85,000 euro flat-rate threshold: if you exceed it you leave the regime from next year, and above 100,000 euro you leave immediately.</li><li><strong>Issue the invoices</strong> for December services on time and check they carry the right wording and stamp duty.</li><li><strong>Check payments</strong> of advances and taxes made with the F24 and keep the receipts.</li><li><strong>Tidy your books</strong>: cash book, receipts and expenses of the year, documents to hand to your accountant.</li><li><strong>Plan purchases</strong> you want to bring forward before year end, if your regime makes them deductible.</li></ul><h2 id="planner">A reminder always at hand</h2><p>Missing a date costs penalties and interest. A <a href="{% include shop-url.html k="prod" id="scadenzario-fiscale-excel" %}">tax deadline planner in Excel</a> flags dates according to your regime, so you do not have to remember them.</p><h2 id="faq">Frequently asked questions</h2><h3>When is the 2026 VAT advance due?</h3><p>On 28 December 2026, because the 27th is a Sunday.</p><h3>Do flat-rate taxpayers pay the VAT advance?</h3><p>No. They do not apply VAT and are not required to pay the advance.</p><h3>When is the 2026 IMU balance due?</h3><p>On 16 December 2026, for owners of non-exempt property.</p><h3>Which tax code is used for the VAT advance?</h3><p>6013 for monthly taxpayers, 6035 for quarterly ones.</p><h3>Below what amount is the VAT advance not due?</h3><p>Below 103.29 euro.</p><div class="art-nota"><p>Tax rules change often and depend on your personal situation.
