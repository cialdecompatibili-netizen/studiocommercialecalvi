<p>A meeting with your accountant is far more productive when your documents are already in order. Fewer requests for additional information, less wasted time and more accurate tax returns from the very first filing. Here is what it is worth preparing, depending on the type of business.</p><h2 id="documenti-di-base">Basic documents for everyone</h2><p>Tax code and a valid ID, the company registry extract if you run a business, and the credentials or delegations needed to access online services (tax drawer, certified email, SPID digital identity). If you are switching firms, also bring last year's returns.</p><h2 id="fatture-e-incassi">Invoices and receipts</h2><p>All invoices issued and received in the period, including credit notes and foreign invoices. Add the till receipts for sales made without an invoice and the statements of your bank accounts, cards and payment platforms: they are needed to reconcile income and payments.</p><h2 id="spese-e-costi">Expenses and costs</h2><p>Gather the expenses linked to your business: rent, utilities, vehicles, training, equipment. Keep the proof of payment together with the invoice, because for some expenses traceability is a condition for the deduction or tax relief.</p><h2 id="contratti-e-finanziamenti">Contracts and loans</h2><p>Lease, leasing, rental and loan agreements, with their amortisation schedules. Also bring insurance policies and any incentives requested or obtained, so the firm can check that they are correctly recorded in the books.</p><h2 id="dipendenti-e-collaboratori">Employees and collaborators</h2><p>If you have staff, you will need contracts, payslips and social security payments. For occasional collaborators or those with a VAT number, bring the invoices or receipts with the related withholding taxes.</p><h2 id="situazione-personale">Personal situation</h2><p>For individuals' income tax returns, medical expenses, mortgage interest, renovation costs and other documented deductions also matter, along with the annual tax certifications (CU) you have received.</p><div class="art-nota"><p>Do not wait for the deadline: bringing your documents at regular intervals, for example every quarter, avoids build-up and lets the firm flag any anomalies or tax-saving opportunities in good time.</p></div>
